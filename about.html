About XBEN

My name is Chukwunyere Emmanuel Chima, popularly known as XBEN. I am a Computer Science graduate from the Federal University of Technology Owerri (FUTO), a Sampling Technician at Zirconex Mining and Metal Laboratory in Abuja, and an ambitious software engineering learner with a growing passion for Artificial Intelligence, cybersecurity, and professional crypto trading.

My journey combines laboratory precision with technology-driven problem solving. Working in mining has strengthened my attention to detail, discipline, and analytical thinking—qualities I now apply to software engineering and AI.

Beyond my technical interests, I am committed to continuous learning, leadership, and building solutions that create real-world impact. My goal is to grow into a world-class software engineer and AI specialist while contributing meaningful innovations in technology.
